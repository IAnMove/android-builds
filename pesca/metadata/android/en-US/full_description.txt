Hold the button to charge your cast, watch fish shadows circle the float and tap in time when one bites. Once hooked, a tap minigame with three bars: time, line and the fish's strength. Casting far brings rarer fish; at night you only see what the lantern lights.

• Casting: A short tap casts near; holding casts far. Every area has a fish school that moves every three hours.
• The bite: The float sinks, the phone buzzes and a sound warns you: you have 1.5 seconds to hook.
• Reeling: Tap inside the fish's circle; the centre is PERFECT. Watch the time, the line and the fish's strength.
• At night: Drag the lantern with your finger: its light attracts the fish.
• Levels, rods and bait: Level progression, a shop, neighbours' orders and named fish.
• While the app is closed: The game moves on while you are away and tells you about it when you come back.

– Portrait, one-handed.
– Offline, no accounts, no ads. Your game is saved on your phone.
– All 3D art and audio are generated by code.
