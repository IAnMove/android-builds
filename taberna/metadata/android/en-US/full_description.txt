A narrative management game. Buy ingredients, brew drinks and cook; open for service and tend the tavern's regulars, each with their own stories, quests and rumours. Between days, the garden, the lake and an optional dungeon expedition.

• Day and night: Prepare by day, serve at night, sleep and start again. You do not need to do everything every day.
• The bar: Orders, trust and chests if you come back alive from the expedition.
• Quests and rumours: Every regular brings stories; a journal keeps them.
• Minigames: Cooking, gardening, lake fishing and a first-person dungeon.
• Spanish and English: Texts and dialogue in both languages.
• Accessibility: Text and HUD size, high contrast, assisted fishing, reduced motion and configurable keys.

– Landscape. PC and Android.
– Offline, no accounts, no ads. Your game and its backups are saved on the device.
– Still in development (v0.1).
