## Full description (EN, ≤4000)

Narciso is run over in front of Clotilde and wakes up in limbo as a lost soul. Along the way he rescues lost souls and throws them at demons; every zone costs life, every soul that dies costs sanity, and at the end you must choose: lead the souls to the light or return to life with Clotilde. A Godot remake of the original game jam game, with its graphics, sounds and font.

• Souls: Tap a soul to rescue it and tap a demon to throw it. Damage grows with every soul attacking together.
• Life and sanity: Moving on costs life; losing souls costs sanity. At zero you stay in limbo or become a demon.
• Two endings: Ten levels with a boss demon and a final choice.
• Spanish and English: Chosen at the start.

– Landscape (2:1). Touch and mouse.
– Offline, no accounts, no ads.
– Prototype. Original game jam game by idelcano; Godot remake.
