A 2D automation game: drills, furnaces, conveyor belts, inserters, power poles and assemblers with recipes; a lab to research, turrets against the bugs and a silo to launch the final rocket. A 160x160 tile map; play with keyboard and mouse or a touch joystick.

• Production chain: Ore, plates, gears, circuits… all the way to the rocket.
• Belts and inserters: Belts, splitters, underground belts and inserters rotated by code.
