A classic room-based roguelike: every floor is generated with treasure, shop, secret and challenge rooms, and ends with a boss. Eight weapons, twenty-one items with combinable effects, four characters, three difficulties, seeds and a daily seed. Every run earns coins for permanent upgrades and achievements that unlock more content.

• Generated floors: Five floors and three biomes with their own enemies and bosses; endless mode after the end.
• Weapons and items: Tears, shotgun, laser, rocket, boomerang, flamethrower, sword… and items that stack.
• Twin sticks: Floating joysticks on mobile, mouse or gamepad on PC. Back button handled.
• Progress: Shop with 18 permanent upgrades, 7 achievements, 4 characters and a daily seed.

– Landscape. Spanish and English.
– Offline, no accounts, no ads.
– Prototype: not tested on a real device. The current name 'Isaac Clone' cannot be used on Play (trademark); 'Endless Basement' is proposed.
