Night has fallen on the forest clearing and the shades are coming in from the edges. Only the bonfire keeps them at bay. You are its keeper: move with your thumb, your flame attacks on its own, and in three minutes the boss arrives.

• One thumb: Drag your finger on the lower half and the joystick appears where you touch. Your flame attacks the nearest shade on its own.
• Light and shadow: Inside the circle of light your attacks hit harder and the shades slow down. Outside, the opposite. Will you risk it?
• Splinters and wood: Shades drop splinters: pick them up to level up and bring them to the bonfire so it never dies.
• Three flames: Spark, heat aura or torch. Pick one of three upgrades on every level.
• The Great Shade: 3-minute runs that end with a boss. Kill it and dawn breaks.
• Ember shop: Between runs, spend embers on permanent upgrades: health, light, wood, damage…
