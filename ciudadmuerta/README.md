## Full description (EN, ≤4000)

An isometric open-world ARPG set in Vallegrande, a present-day city taken by the plague. Map, combat, loot, skills, quests and events; hordes that come at night and a horde mode to survive in the industrial arena.

• Vallegrande: An open city with districts, camps, bonfires you discover and fast travel between them.
• Combat and loot: Attack the nearest, use potions and skills, equip what you find from your bag.
• Quests and events: Errands, enemy groups popping up on the map and hordes when night falls.
• Horde mode: Ever bigger waves until dawn. Pick an upgrade on every level.
• Characters: Several classes, several saved games and a day-night cycle.
• Spanish and English: Interface in both languages; works with touch, keyboard and mouse or gamepad.

– Landscape. PC and Android.
– Offline, no accounts, no ads. Saved games stay on the device.
– A playable base (v0.1) meant to keep growing. Most art is AI-generated; the sound is generated by code.
