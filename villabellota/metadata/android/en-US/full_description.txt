You arrive on the island and Don Topo welcomes you in the square with a net, a rod and 1,000 acorns. From then on the island follows your phone's time, day and season: 28 fish and 25 bugs by month and hour, new fossils every day, cherry blossoms in April and snow in winter. Sell at Rufina's shop, donate to Doña Tortuga's museum, upgrade your house, craft furniture at the workbench and win over Pipa, Bruno, Lola and Coco. Visitors drop by the square every week and the calendar brings contests, fireworks and festivals.

• Real clock: Your device's time, day and season. Come back after a few days and they tell you what changed.
• Fishing and bugs: 28 fish and 25 bugs by month, hour and place. Tiptoe or they flee.
• Dig and plant: Fossils, clams, mineral rocks; seeds, cross-breeding flowers and saplings.
• Shop and museum: Sell your catch, buy tools and furniture, donate one of every species.
• Your home: From a tent to a house with a mailbox; 46 pieces of furniture to place and turn. Interest-free loan.
• Neighbours: Four neighbours with schedules, voices, errands and tastes. Friendship grows by chatting and gifting.
• Festivals and visitors: Fishing and bug contests, fireworks, a spooky night in October, a winter festival... And every week, visitors who pay well or sell rare things.
• Crafting and clothes: 17 recipes wash up in bottles on the beach: furniture, tools and hats. 17 hats and tops to dress your way.
• Mushroom market: Buy mushrooms on Sunday and sell them during the week when the price goes up. Next Sunday they go bad!

– Landscape. PC and Android.
– Offline, no accounts, no ads. Your game is saved on the device.
– Optional reminders on Android (fruit, festivals, mushrooms): they ask for the notification permission.
– Mechanics inspired by cozy island-life games; names, characters and art are original and generated by code.
