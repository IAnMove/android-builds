## Full description (EN, ≤4000)

An island of about 1.8 km² with beaches, hills, forests, mountains and a plateau, all generated by code. There is a village, a giant, errands, a race, a firefly lantern and a lost flock. Each experience is a mod you can switch on or off before or during play.

• Brumacosta: Follow the path from the beach to the village: six houses, a market, a mill, a bonfire and five villagers.
• Brum the giant: Talk to him and jump over his three golden waves. Winning unlocks a leap into the sky.
• The broom: Grab it and fly up to 260 m. Automatic landing when you come down.
• The firefly lantern: Scatter shades with flashes, recover the sparks and free a companion that lets you glide.
• The lost flock: Get the whistle, gather Nube, Mora and Trufa and guide them to the pen. Wolves are lurking.
• Race and errands: Pass six gates against the clock and deliver flowers to Lía. Records are kept.

– Landscape. Touch controls on mobile; keyboard and mouse on PC.
– Offline, no accounts, no ads. Your game is saved on the device.
– All art is generated by code. Version 0.1: still in development.

Made by IAnMove with Godot Engine. No ads, no accounts, no internet connection.
