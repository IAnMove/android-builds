## Full description (EN, ≤4000)

An 81x81 map with a circular river and four fords. Raise walls by dragging, place towers, cannons, musketeers and explosive barrels, dig moats and lay bridges. Orcs follow a flow field: they go around walls when the detour is short and knock them down otherwise; catapults arrive on wave 4 and gunpowder unlocks on wave 5. Buildings crack, collapse with physics and leave rubble.

• Build with a finger: Tap to build; hold and drag to paint walls. Two fingers move, turn and zoom the camera.
• Fifteen waves: Grunts, runt swarms, brutes and catapults; wave 15 brings more than 200 orcs.
• Water: Swimming slows orcs; brutes drown. Moats and bridges change their paths.
• Destruction: Staged building damage, physics rubble, dust and camera shake.
